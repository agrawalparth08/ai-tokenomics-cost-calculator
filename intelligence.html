3. Cost per point of intelligence

Capability score is blank by default. Enter a score you trust, for example your own pass rate from the eval above, on a 0 to 100 scale, or a benchmark you use for this kind of task. We do not ship benchmark numbers here, because a score that fits your workload is worth more than a generic leaderboard number.

Enter at least two scores to rank models by cost per point.

Blended cost per task uses the same workload as the eval panel above: input tokens × input price + output tokens × output price, divided by 1,000,000. Cost per point = cost per task ÷ your score. Lower is better on both. A model can win on cost per task and lose on cost per point, that gap is the whole point of this tool: a cheaper model at a lower score can cost more per point than a pricier model that is disproportionately more capable.
