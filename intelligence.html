Calculator 7 · price per point of intelligence

Cheapest per token is not the same as cheapest per point.

A model that costs less per million tokens can still cost more per unit of work, if it needs more tokens, more retries, or is simply worse at the task and you end up paying a human to fix it. This tool does not tell you which model is smarter, we do not ship benchmark numbers. Enter at least two scores to rank models by cost per point.

Blended cost per task = (input tokens × input price + output tokens × output price) ÷ 1,000,000. Cost per point = cost per task ÷ your score. Lower is better on both. A model can win on cost per task and lose on cost per point, that gap is the whole point of this tool: a cheaper model at a lower score can cost more per point than a pricier model that is disproportionately more capable.
