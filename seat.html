The $200 seat was never the real price. Usage was always billed underneath it.

A flat seat price feels like a fixed cost. It is not, if usage is billed separately once you go over what the seat covers. One heavy user can turn a $200 a month seat into a $2,000 a month bill without anyone changing the plan. Slide your own numbers, including a per person cap, and see where you land.

Bill per person = seat price + usage cost, where usage cost = tokens × overage price ÷ 1,000,000. With a cap, usage cost is capped at the per person monthly cap you set above; the seat price itself is never capped. Team totals multiply the tier bill by the number of people on the plan, as if everyone on the team used that tier that month.
