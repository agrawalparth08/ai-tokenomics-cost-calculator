A chat model has no memory between calls. So every turn, the app sends the whole conversation so far, from the start, again. Your new message is a small part of that. The rest is history you already paid for once, being paid for again. This is the context window tax. Slide your own numbers and see how big it is for you.

Why this happens

A chat model is stateless. It does not remember your last message; the app has to hand it the entire transcript again on every call, then add the new turn on top. Turn by turn, the history grows and the resend grows with it, even though the number of things you actually typed grows in a straight line. That gap between what you typed and what got billed is the context window tax. It shows up fastest with a long system prompt, big attached documents, or a chat that just runs a long time. Caching does not remove the tax. It just charges a lower rate on the part that has not changed since last time.
